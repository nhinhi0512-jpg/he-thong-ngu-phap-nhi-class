📄 Bài 1: Coronavirus

The coronavirus is a kind of virus. Viruses are tiny germs that are so small that you can't see them. They are so (1) that they can float through the air in tiny drops of water, and they can sit on your skin without you feeling them. If some of these germs get inside you, they can use your body to make more germs, and that can make you ill.

There are lots of different sorts (2) coronavirus and some of them infect people. If you have been infected with one of these coronaviruses, all you probably (3) was a snotty nose or a cough.

But when this completely new coronavirus germ gets inside a human body, it causes (4) illness called COVID-19. When people talk about "catching the coronavirus", they are talking about this illness. Because this coronavirus is new, scientists don't know everything about it yet. But they think that there are two main (5) that people can catch it.

1. (1) ______

🔍 "Tiny" → nhỏ, và "so small that you can't see them" → small là phù hợp nhất.

2. (2) ______

📘 Cấu trúc "sorts of" = các loại của. "different sorts of coronavirus".

3. (3) ______

🧬 "all you probably had" – had a snotty nose or a cough (bị sổ mũi hoặc ho).

4. (4) ______

📚 "illness" bắt đầu bằng nguyên âm "i" → dùng mạo từ "an".

5. (5) ______

🦠 "two main ways that people can catch it" – hai cách chính mà mọi người có thể nhiễm.

6. (6) ______

7. (7) ______

8. (8) ______

⚠️ "increases the size of the problems" = gia tăng quy mô các vấn đề.

9. (9) ______

10. (10) ______

📌 Đại từ quan hệ "that" thay thế cho "solutions" – many solutions that can be used.

📄 Bài 3: Music and Health

Most people enjoy listening to music but few realise the important and largely positive effects it can have on us. We know that certain (11) of music are used to influence our emotions and our behaviour. For example, airlines use soothing music before a flight to (12) passengers, especially those who may feel nervous about flying. You may have noticed how shops often play fast, rousing music – this tends to make us feel (13) and more likely to spend money!

Music is also being used now as a psychiatric therapy. It seems to be particularly (14) for eating disorders and addictions, but also for sufferers of post-traumatic stress syndrome. People attending group therapy sessions are invited to bring along their favourite tracks. Not everyone does, but those (15) do so play them for the group. This creates a sense of belonging, as well as a more relaxed atmosphere for the therapy session.

🎵 "certain forms of music" = những hình thức âm nhạc nhất định.

12. (12) ______

13. (13) ______

🎶 Fast, rousing music → khiến ta cảm thấy vui vẻ (happier) và dễ tiêu tiền hơn.

14. (14) ______

💊 "particularly useful for eating disorders" = đặc biệt hữu ích cho rối loạn ăn uống.

15. (15) ______

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 "those who do so" – đại từ quan hệ "who" thay thế cho người.

📄 Bài 4: Culture Shock

Many people dream of living in a foreign country. It can be an amazing experience for those (16) are willing to settle down in a new place. (17) there's one potential problem you should be aware of: culture shock. Culture shock is the feeling we get from living in a place that is so different to where we grew up that we are not sure (18) to deal with it. Societies are (19) in many different ways. Customs and traditions can be very different and that can sometimes make it difficult to get on with local people who might not approve of things you do and might object to things you say. You might be banned from doing things in another country that are perfectly legal in your own. For example, in Singapore people can be forced to (20) a large fine just for dropping rubbish. Eventually, though, most people who live abroad fall in love with their adopted country and learn to accept its differences.

16. (16) ______

🧑 "those who are willing" – đại từ quan hệ "who" thay thế cho người.

17. (17) ______

⚠️ "However" – tuy nhiên, có một vấn đề tiềm ẩn (thể hiện sự đối lập).

18. (18) ______

🤔 "not sure how to deal with it" – không biết làm thế nào để đối phó.

19. (19) ______

🏛️ "Societies are organized" – xã hội được tổ chức (dạng bị động, quá khứ phân từ).

20. (20) ______

💰 "pay a large fine" – trả một khoản tiền phạt lớn.

🔢 Dạng 2: Sắp xếp câu thành đoạn văn hoàn chỉnh

✅ Sắp xếp các câu A, B, C, D, E theo thứ tự đúng để tạo thành một đoạn văn có nghĩa.

📄 Bài 1: Mark Twain

Hãy sắp xếp các câu sau thành đoạn văn hoàn chỉnh:

A. By 1887, the 49-year-old author had invested most of his money in one project, the Paige typesetting machine.
B. He worked hard to take care of his problems by producing a number of books in a row.
C. Twain expected the Paige machine to be completed in 1889, but it was never finished.
D. From the start of his career, Mark Twain was fascinated with inventions and spent much of his income on them without much success.
E. He wrote several books, but they didn't solve his financial problems, and he was forced to declare bankruptcy.
✅ Đáp án đúng: A, C, D, B, E.
Giải thích: D (giới thiệu chung) → A (chi tiết về đầu tư) → C (kỳ vọng và thất bại) → B (nỗ lực viết sách) → E (kết quả thất bại).

📄 Bài 2: Sydney

Hãy sắp xếp các câu sau thành đoạn văn hoàn chỉnh:

A. Sydney is Australia's most exciting city.
B. There are over 20 excellent beaches close to Sydney and its warm climate has made it a favorite city for immigrants.
C. The Sydney Harbor Bridge was built in 1932 and the Opera House was opened in 1973.
D. The history of Australia begins here with Captain Arthur Philips' arrival in 1788.
E. Sydney is the biggest city in Australia, the busiest port in the South Pacific, and one of the most beautiful cities in the world.
✅ Đáp án đúng: A, D, B, C, E.
Giải thích: A (giới thiệu chung) → D (lịch sử) → B (đặc điểm địa lý, khí hậu) → C (các công trình nổi tiếng) → E (kết luận tổng quan).

📄 Bài 3: E-commerce

Hãy sắp xếp các câu sau thành đoạn văn hoàn chỉnh:

A. In recent years, e-commerce has grown exponentially, transforming the way people shop.
B. However, e-commerce also faces challenges, such as shipping costs and the inability to examine products before purchase.
C. Online shopping offers convenience, allowing customers to purchase products from home.
D. Despite these drawbacks, the trend shows no signs of slowing down.
E. With mobile shopping and personalized recommendations, the e-commerce industry continues to expand rapidly.
✅ Đáp án đúng: A, C, B, D, E.
Giải thích: A (mở đầu) → C (lợi ích) → B (thách thức) → D (tuy nhiên) → E (kết luận tích cực).

📄 Bài 4: Sleep

Hãy sắp xếp các câu sau thành đoạn văn hoàn chỉnh:

A. During sleep, our bodies repair cells, consolidate memories, and release essential hormones.
B. Sleep is a vital part of our daily lives, yet many people underestimate its importance.
C. Lack of sleep can lead to heart disease, diabetes, and affects cognitive functions.
D. Studies show that adults need between seven and nine hours of sleep per night.
E. Modern lifestyles often reduce sleep time, with many people sacrificing sleep without realizing the consequences.
✅ Đáp án đúng: B, A, D, C, E.
Giải thích: B (mở đầu) → A (lợi ích của giấc ngủ) → D (nhu cầu) → C (hậu quả) → E (vấn đề hiện đại).

📄 Bài 1: Newcastle

I was born in Newcastle, a city in the North East of England. Newcastle is on the bank of the River Tyne. It is quite big, with a population of about 200,000 people. There is a cathedral and a university. There are five bridges over the River Tyne, which link Newcastle to the next town, Gateshead, where there is one of the biggest shopping centers in the world.

A few years ago, the main industries were shipping and coal mining, but now the chemical and soap industries are more important.

I left Newcastle ten years ago but I often return. I miss the people, who are very friendly, and I miss the beautiful countryside near the city, where there are so many hills and streams.

People who are born near the River Tyne have a special name. They are called "Geodies". I am very pleased to be called a "Geodies".

1. Where is Newcastle located?

📍 Bài đọc: "Newcastle, a city in the North East of England" → đáp án A.

2. What is the approximate population of Newcastle?

📊 Bài đọc: "with a population of about 200,000 people" → around 200,000.

3. What were the primary industries in Newcastle in the past?

⚓ Bài đọc: "the main industries were shipping and coal mining" → đáp án D.

4. How would you describe the people of Newcastle?

👥 Bài đọc: "the people, who are very friendly" → đáp án C.

5. What are the natives of the River Tyne area called?

🏷️ Bài đọc: "They are called 'Geodies'" → đáp án D.

📄 Bài 2: Brazil

Brazil, the largest country in South America and the fifth largest country in the world, is located in the eastern half of South America. The people of Brazil are famous for their outgoing, friendly, and fun-loving nature. Brazilian women are considered to be among the best dressed in the world.

A number of industrial products are produced in Brazil, including cars, chemicals, ships, machines and military weapons. Mining is also an important industry. Agriculture is another important industry. Many crops are exported including coffee (Brazil is the largest coffee grower in the world), cotton, soybeans, sugar, cocoa, rice, corn and fruit.

6. Where is Brazil situated?

8. How are the people of Brazil characterized?

9. What is Brazil's most significant agricultural export product?

🌍 Bài đọc: "the fifth largest country in the world" → đáp án A.

📄 Bài 3: Sydney

13. Which statement about Sydney is NOT true according to the passage?

❌ Bài đọc: "made it a favorite city for immigrants" → A sai.

14. How many beaches are located near Sydney?

🏖️ Bài đọc: "There are over 20 excellent beaches" → đáp án B.

15. When was the Sydney Harbor Bridge constructed?

📄 Bài 4: Homelessness

Homelessness is a problem in many big cities in the world. It occurs when a part of the population does not have any fixed place to call home. Hundreds of millions of people in the world spend at least some time of the year homeless. This is an issue that the authorities are trying to deal with. However, there are more things that each person can do to help those people. One of the things that you can do to help the homeless is to volunteer your time. If you have a lot of free time, you might go on an extended trip to help set up homes or improve an impoverished area. Even with just a few hours a week, it is possible to make an impact in your own city. You can also sign up to help at a local soup kitchen: most cities have a mission of some kind helping food to the homeless and offering temporary shelter.

16. What causes homelessness?

🏠 Bài đọc: "does not have any fixed place to call home" → đáp án C.

17. What does the word "This" in the passage refer to?

📌 "This" thay thế cho "Homelessness" – vấn đề được đề cập ngay trước đó.

18. What could you do if you have plenty of free time?

✈️ Bài đọc: "go on an extended trip to help set up homes" → đáp án A.

19. What does the phrase "sign up" most likely mean?

20. What do most cities offer to the homeless?

🏚️ Bài đọc: "offering temporary shelter" → đáp án B.
