2. They (buy) ________ a beautiful new house in London last month.
3. She (not / visit) ________ her grandmother last weekend.
4. We (see) ________ an interesting documentary on TV two days ago.
5. My brother (finish) ________ his university degree in 2024.

📝 3.9 Viết lại câu hoàn chỉnh không đổi nghĩa

25. In the middle of our video game, the power cut out. → We ________________________ when the power cut out.
26. Peter fell asleep during the long boring lecture. → While Peter ________________________, he fell asleep.
27. I started reading at 7 PM. The doorbell rang at 7:30 PM. → I ________________________ when the doorbell rang.
28. Driving home, Mary saw a beautiful rainbow. → Mary saw a beautiful rainbow while she ________________________.
