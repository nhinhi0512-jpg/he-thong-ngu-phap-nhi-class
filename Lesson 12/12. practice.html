🔗 Luyện tập: Câu ghép (Compound Sentences)

✅ Chọn liên từ kết hợp (FANBOYS) phù hợp để hoàn thành câu ghép.

1. She wanted to go out, _______ it was raining.

☔ Hai ý trái ngược → dùng "but".

2. I was tired, _______ I went to bed early.

😴 Mệt → đi ngủ sớm (kết quả) → "so".

3. He doesn't like coffee, _______ does he like tea.

🚫 "Nor" nối hai mệnh đề phủ định.

4. You can have cake _______ ice cream for dessert.

🍰 Lựa chọn giữa hai món → "or".

5. She is very young, _______ she is very wise.

🧠 Tương phản bất ngờ → "yet".

6. He stayed home, _______ he was feeling unwell.

🤒 "For" = bởi vì (giải thích lý do).

7. They wanted to go on a picnic, _______ the weather was bad.

🌧️ Mong muốn nhưng thời tiết xấu → "but".

8. I was late, _______ I missed the bus.

🚌 Trễ → lỡ xe buýt (kết quả) → "so".

9. He didn't study, _______ he passed the exam.

📝 Không học nhưng vẫn đỗ → "yet".

10. You can stay here, _______ you can go home.

🚪 Lựa chọn giữa hai hành động → "or".

11. She is intelligent, _______ she lacks experience.

🧠 Thông minh nhưng thiếu kinh nghiệm → "but".

12. I like tea, _______ he likes coffee.

☕ Thêm thông tin tương đồng → "and".

13. We can go to the beach, _______ we can stay home.

🏖️ Hai lựa chọn → "or".

14. I want to go, _______ I'm too busy.

⏳ Muốn đi nhưng bận → "but".

15. She speaks English, _______ she doesn't speak French.

🗣️ Nói tiếng Anh nhưng không nói tiếng Pháp → "but".

16. He didn't eat breakfast, _______ he was hungry.

🍽️ Không ăn sáng nên đói → "so".

17. She studied hard, _______ she passed the test.

📚 Học chăm nên đỗ → "so".

18. He is rich, _______ he is not happy.

💰 Giàu nhưng không hạnh phúc → "but".

19. She can sing, _______ she can dance.

🎤 Vừa hát vừa nhảy → "and".

20. He didn't call, _______ did he send a message.

📱 Không gọi cũng không nhắn → "nor".

21. She was tired, _______ she kept working.

😓 Mệt nhưng vẫn làm việc → "yet".

22. I woke up late, _______ I missed the bus.

⏰ Dậy muộn nên lỡ xe → "so".

23. He loves her, _______ she doesn't love him.

💔 Yêu nhưng không được đáp lại → "but".

24. We can go now, _______ we can wait.

⏳ Lựa chọn đi ngay hay chờ → "or".

25. She is both smart _______ hardworking.

🧠 Both... and → "and".

🧩 Luyện tập: Câu phức (Complex Sentences)

✅ Chọn liên từ phụ thuộc phù hợp để hoàn thành câu phức.

1. _______ it was raining, we stayed inside.

🌧️ Lý do → "Because".

2. We stayed inside _______ it was raining.

🌧️ Lý do → "because".

3. _______ she was tired, she finished her homework.

😓 Mặc dù mệt vẫn làm bài → "Although".

4. She finished her homework _______ she was tired.

😓 Mặc dù mệt → "although".

5. _______ you study, you will pass the exam.

📚 Điều kiện → "If".

6. He called me _______ I was eating.

🍽️ Thời điểm → "when".

7. _______ it was late, we went home.

🌙 Lý do → "Because".

8. She saved money _______ she could travel.

✈️ Mục đích → "so that".

9. _______ the weather was bad, they went on a picnic.

🌧️ Mặc dù thời tiết xấu → "Although".

10. We went out _______ it was raining.

🌧️ Mặc dù mưa vẫn đi → "although".

11. _______ you don't study, you will fail.

📚 Điều kiện → "If".

12. He left _______ I arrived.

⏰ Thời gian → "before".

13. She called me _______ I had left.

⏰ Thời gian → "after".

14. _______ he was hungry, he ate a sandwich.

🍽️ Lý do → "Because".

15. He ate a sandwich _______ he was hungry.

🍽️ Lý do → "because".

16. _______ she was sick, she went to school.

🤒 Mặc dù ốm vẫn đi học → "Although".

17. We waited _______ he arrived.

⏳ Thời gian → "until".

18. _______ you are ready, we will start.

⏰ Thời điểm → "When".

19. He studied hard _______ he could get a scholarship.

🎓 Mục đích → "so that".

20. _______ it was sunny, we stayed inside.

☀️ Mặc dù trời nắng vẫn ở trong → "Although".

21. _______ you apologize, I won't forgive you.

😤 Trừ khi xin lỗi → "Unless".

22. She was singing _______ she was cooking.

🎵 Cùng lúc → "while".

23. _______ you finish your homework, you can watch TV.

📺 Thời gian → "After".

24. He ran fast _______ he could catch the train.

🚄 Mục đích → "so that".

25. _______ he is rich, he is not happy.

💰 Mặc dù giàu không hạnh phúc → "Although".

🎯 Luyện tập tổng hợp: Câu ghép & Câu phức

✅ Chọn đáp án đúng (có thể là liên từ kết hợp hoặc phụ thuộc).

1. She was tired, _______ she went to bed early.

😴 Mệt nên đi ngủ → "so" (câu ghép).

2. _______ she was tired, she went to bed early.

😴 Vì mệt nên đi ngủ → "Because" (câu phức).

3. He is rich, _______ he is not happy.

💰 Giàu nhưng không hạnh phúc → "but" (câu ghép).

4. _______ he is rich, he is not happy.

💰 Mặc dù giàu không hạnh phúc → "Although" (câu phức).

5. I woke up late, _______ I missed the bus.

⏰ Dậy muộn nên lỡ xe → "so" (câu ghép).

6. _______ I woke up late, I missed the bus.

⏰ Vì dậy muộn nên lỡ xe → "Because" (câu phức).

7. You can have tea _______ coffee.

☕ Lựa chọn → "or" (câu ghép).

8. She studied hard, _______ she passed the test.

📚 Học chăm nên đỗ → "so" (câu ghép).

9. _______ she studied hard, she passed the test.

📚 Vì học chăm nên đỗ → "Because" (câu phức).

10. He doesn't like tea, _______ does he like coffee.

🚫 Không thích cả hai → "nor" (câu ghép).

11. _______ it was raining, we went out.

🌧️ Mặc dù mưa vẫn đi → "Although" (câu phức).

12. We went out _______ it was raining.

🌧️ Mặc dù mưa → "although" (câu phức).

13. She is young, _______ she is very wise.

🧠 Trẻ nhưng khôn ngoan → "yet" (câu ghép).

14. _______ you work hard, you will succeed.

💪 Điều kiện → "If" (câu phức).

15. He called me _______ I was sleeping.

😴 Cùng lúc → "while" (câu phức).

16. She wanted to go, _______ she was too busy.

⏳ Muốn đi nhưng bận → "but" (câu ghép).

17. _______ she was busy, she helped me.

🤝 Mặc dù bận vẫn giúp → "Although" (câu phức).

18. I like tea, _______ he likes coffee.

☕ Thêm thông tin → "and" (câu ghép).

19. She not only sings _______ also dances.

🎤 Not only... but also → "but" (câu ghép).

20. _______ you apologize, I won't forgive you.

😤 Trừ khi xin lỗi → "Unless" (câu phức).

21. He is both smart _______ hardworking.

🧠 Both... and → "and" (câu ghép).

22. She stayed home _______ she was sick.

🤒 Vì ốm → "because" (câu phức).

23. She was sick, _______ she stayed home.

🤒 Ốm nên ở nhà → "so" (câu ghép).

24. We waited _______ he arrived.

⏳ Chờ đến khi → "until" (câu phức).

25. She ran fast, _______ she caught the train.

🏃 Chạy nhanh nên bắt kịp → "so" (câu ghép).
