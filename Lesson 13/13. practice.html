⚖️ Luyện tập: So sánh bằng

✅ Chọn đáp án đúng để hoàn thành câu so sánh bằng.

1. She is _______ her brother.

📏 So sánh bằng: as + adj + as.

2. He runs _______ his friend.

🏃 So sánh bằng: as + adv + as.

3. She is not _______ her mother.

📏 Phủ định: not as/so + adj + as.

4. I have _______ money _______ you.

💰 as much + danh từ không đếm được + as.

5. She has _______ books _______ I do.

📚 as many + danh từ đếm được số nhiều + as.

6. This film is not _______ interesting _______ the book.

🎬 Phủ định: not as/so + adj + as.

7. She speaks English _______ her teacher.

🗣️ as + adv + as (trạng từ).

8. He is _______ his father.

🎂 as + adj + as.

9. She doesn't earn _______ money _______ her brother.

💰 as much + danh từ không đếm được.

10. This box is _______ that one.

📦 as + adj + as.

11. She sings _______ her sister.

🎤 as + adv + as.

12. He doesn't work _______ his colleague.

💼 Phủ định: not as/so + adv + as.

13. This task is _______ that one.

📋 as + adj + as.

14. She has _______ experience _______ her manager.

💼 as much + danh từ không đếm được.

15. He can jump _______ his brother.

🏀 as + adj + as.

16. The weather today is not _______ yesterday.

☀️ Phủ định: not as/so + adj + as.

17. She dressed _______ her friend.

👗 as + adv + as.

18. This laptop is _______ that one.

💻 as + adj + as.

20. She ran _______ her competitor.

🏃 as + adv + as.

📈 Luyện tập: So sánh hơn

✅ Chọn đáp án đúng để hoàn thành câu so sánh hơn.

1. He is _______ his brother.

📏 tall → taller than (adj ngắn).

2. She runs _______ me.

🏃 fast → faster than (adv ngắn).

3. This book is _______ that one.

📖 interesting (3 âm) → more interesting than.

4. He speaks _______ his sister.

🗣️ fluently (adv dài) → more fluently than.

5. She has _______ money than I do.

💰 little → less (so sánh hơn).

6. He is _______ his friend.

🧠 smart (1 âm) → smarter than.

7. This task is _______ that one.

📋 difficult (3 âm) → more difficult than.

8. She runs _______ her brother.

🐢 slowly (adv dài) → more slowly than.

9. He has _______ books than I do.

📚 many → more (so sánh hơn).

10. She is _______ her sister.

🌸 beautiful (3 âm) → more beautiful than.

11. He drives _______ his friend.

🚗 carefully (adv dài) → more carefully than.

12. This dress is _______ that one.

👗 cheap (1 âm) → cheaper than.

13. He is _______ his father.

📏 tall → taller than.

14. She speaks English _______ her brother.

🗣️ well → better than (bất quy tắc).

15. This problem is _______ that one.

🧩 easy → easier than.

16. He is _______ his colleague.

💼 hardworking (4 âm) → more hardworking than.

17. She has _______ experience than her manager.

💼 little → less (so sánh hơn).

18. He ran _______ his friend.

🏃 quickly (adv dài) → more quickly than.

19. This car is _______ that one.

🚗 fast → faster than.

20. She is _______ her sister.

😊 outgoing (3 âm) → more outgoing than.

🏆 Luyện tập: So sánh nhất

✅ Chọn đáp án đúng để hoàn thành câu so sánh nhất.

1. She is _______ student in the class.

🏆 good → the best (so sánh nhất).

2. This is _______ dress I have ever seen.

👗 the most + adj dài (so sánh nhất).

3. That was _______ day of my life.

😊 happy → the happiest (so sánh nhất).

4. This is _______ expensive car in the showroom.

🚗 the most + adj (so sánh nhất).

5. She sings _______ of all.

🎤 the most + adv (so sánh nhất).

6. He is _______ person I know.

🤝 the most + adj (so sánh nhất).

7. This is _______ story I've ever read.

📖 the most + adj (so sánh nhất).

8. She has _______ experience in the team.

💼 little → the least (so sánh nhất).

9. He works _______ of all.

💪 hard → the hardest (so sánh nhất).

10. This is _______ film I have ever watched.

🎬 bad → the worst (so sánh nhất).

11. She is _______ student in the school.

🧠 the most + adj (so sánh nhất).

12. He has _______ money of all.

💰 many/much → the most (so sánh nhất).

13. This is _______ book in the library.

📚 old → the oldest (so sánh nhất).

14. She runs _______ in the team.

🏃 fast → the fastest (so sánh nhất).

15. This is _______ mountain in the world.

⛰️ high → the highest (so sánh nhất).

16. He is _______ man I have ever met.

🤗 kind → the kindest (so sánh nhất).

17. She speaks English _______ of all.

🗣️ the most + adv (so sánh nhất).

18. This is _______ decision I have made.

🤔 the most + adj (so sánh nhất).

19. He is _______ singer in the competition.

🎤 good → the best (so sánh nhất).

20. This is _______ hotel in the city.

🏨 the most + adj (so sánh nhất).

✨ Luyện tập: So sánh đặc biệt

✅ Chọn đáp án đúng cho bội số, cấp tiến, so sánh kép.

1. This box is _______ that one.

📦 Bội số: twice as + adj + as.

2. My salary is _______ his.

💰 Bội số: three times as + adj + as.

3. He is _______ his brother.

🎂 Bội số: half as + adj + as.

4. It's getting _______ and _______.

❄️ Cấp tiến (adj ngắn): adj-er and adj-er.

5. She becomes _______ beautiful.

🌸 Cấp tiến (adj dài): more and more + adj.

6. The weather is getting _______ and _______.

🌧️ bad → worse (cấp tiến).

7. _______ you study, _______ you become.

📚 So sánh kép: The + comparative, the + comparative.

8. _______ you run, _______ you feel.

🏃 So sánh kép với trạng từ và tính từ.

9. _______ you wait, _______ it will be.

⏳ So sánh kép: The + comparative, the + comparative.

10. _______ you eat, _______ you get.

🍔 So sánh kép: The + comparative, the + comparative.

11. The city is _______ crowded than before.

🏙️ Bội số: twice as + adj + as.

12. She is getting _______ and _______.

💪 Cấp tiến: more and more + adj dài.

13. The situation is getting _______ and _______.

📉 bad → worse (cấp tiến).

14. _______ you practice, _______ you improve.

🎯 So sánh kép: The + comparative, the + comparative.

15. He is _______ his father.

📏 Bội số: twice as + adj + as.

16. The days are getting _______ and _______.

☀️ long → longer (cấp tiến).

17. _______ you go, _______ you see.

🗺️ So sánh kép với bất quy tắc: further/more.

18. She has _______ books than I do.

📚 Bội số với danh từ đếm được: three times as many.

19. _______ you save, _______ you have.

💰 So sánh kép: The + comparative, the + comparative.

20. This room is _______ that one.

🛋️ Bội số: three times as + adj + as.

🎯 Luyện tập tổng hợp: Tất cả các dạng so sánh
