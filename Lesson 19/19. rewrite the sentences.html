📚 Bài 19: Viết lại câu & Câu đồng nghĩa

Sentence Transformation & Synonyms
✍️ Làm bài tập

🔹 Câu đồng nghĩa (Synonyms)

💡 Câu đồng nghĩa là câu có cùng ý nghĩa với câu gốc nhưng được diễn đạt bằng từ vựng hoặc cấu trúc khác.

📌 Các dạng câu đồng nghĩa thường gặp

1. Dùng từ đồng nghĩa (synonyms) Thay thế từ này bằng từ khác có cùng nghĩa. She is beautiful. → She is pretty. He is wealthy. → He is rich.
2. Dùng trái nghĩa (antonyms) với phủ định Thay từ bằng từ trái nghĩa và thêm phủ định. It is not difficult. → It is easy. She isn't short. → She is tall.
3. Dùng cấu trúc tương đương Thay đổi cấu trúc ngữ pháp nhưng giữ nguyên nghĩa. No one is as tall as him. → He is the tallest. She is too young to drive. → She is not old enough to drive.

📌 Các dạng câu đồng nghĩa đặc biệt

4. Đổi So sánh hơn ⇔ So sánh nhất No other student is as tall as Nam. → Nam is the tallest student. She is the most beautiful girl. → No other girl is as beautiful as her.
5. Đổi Because ⇔ Because of Because the weather was bad, we stayed home. → Because of the bad weather, we stayed home.
6. Đổi Although ⇔ In spite of / Despite Although he is rich, he is unhappy. → Despite being rich, he is unhappy.
Ví dụ chi tiết:
No other metal is as expensive as gold. (Không kim loại nào đắt bằng vàng.)
→ Gold is the most expensive of all metals. (Vàng là đắt nhất trong tất cả các kim loại.)

People use computers for various purposes. (Mọi người dùng máy tính cho nhiều mục đích.)
→ Computers are used by people for various purposes. (Máy tính được dùng cho nhiều mục đích.)
📌 Mẹo: Khi tìm câu đồng nghĩa, hãy xác định nghĩa chính của câu gốc, sau đó tìm câu nào diễn đạt lại nghĩa đó một cách chính xác nhất.

🔸 Cấu trúc viết lại câu

💡 Đây là các cấu trúc thường xuất hiện trong bài tập viết lại câu. Nắm vững chúng sẽ giúp bạn làm bài nhanh và chính xác.

📌 Nhóm 1: Cấu trúc cơ bản

1. Chủ động ⇔ Bị động Đổi chủ ngữ và tân ngữ, chia động từ ở dạng bị động. They built this house in 1990. → This house was built in 1990. People speak English all over the world. → English is spoken all over the world.
2. Trực tiếp ⇔ Gián tiếp (câu tường thuật) Lùi thì, đổi đại từ, trạng từ chỉ thời gian/nơi chốn. He said, "I am happy." → He said that he was happy. "I will call you tomorrow," she said. → She said she would call me the next day.
3. Câu hỏi Yes/No ⇔ Tường thuật Dùng if/whether, lùi thì, đổi đại từ. "Do you like coffee?" he asked. → He asked if I liked coffee.
4. Câu hỏi Wh- ⇔ Tường thuật Giữ nguyên từ hỏi, lùi thì, đổi đại từ. "Where do you live?" she asked. → She asked where I lived.

📌 Nhóm 2: Cấu trúc so sánh

5. So sánh bằng ⇔ So sánh hơn/nhất as...as = not as...as, so sánh hơn, so sánh nhất. She is as tall as her sister. → Her sister is as tall as she is. No one is taller than him. → He is the tallest.
6. So...that / Such...that So + adj/adv + that / Such + (a/an) + adj + N + that. The day was so cold that we stayed indoors. → It was such a cold day that we stayed indoors.
7. Too...to / Enough...to Too + adj + (for O) + to V = quá...đến nỗi không thể. He is too young to drive. → He is not old enough to drive. The box is too heavy for me to lift. → The box is not light enough for me to lift.

📌 Nhóm 3: Thì và động từ

8. Hiện tại hoàn thành ⇔ Quá khứ đơn Cách 1: S + have/has + V3 + for/since... ⇔ S + V(quá khứ)... I have lived here for 5 years. → I moved here 5 years ago. It is 3 years since I last saw her. → I haven't seen her for 3 years. She started working here in 2010. → She has worked here since 2010. Lưu ý: It is + khoảng thời gian + since + S + V(quá khứ) = S + have/has + not + V3 + for + khoảng thời gian.
9. Used to / Be used to / Get used to Used to + V = thói quen quá khứ (không còn ở hiện tại). When I was a child, I went to the park every Saturday. → I used to go to the park every Saturday. They usually walk in the park after dinner. → They are used to walking in the park after dinner.
10. It takes / spends It takes + O + time + to V = S + spend + time + V-ing. It took him two hours to do his homework. → He spent two hours doing his homework.

📌 Nhóm 4: Liên từ và mệnh đề

11. Because ⇔ Because of Because + S + V = Because of + N/V-ing. He was late because the traffic was heavy. → He was late because of the heavy traffic.
12. Although ⇔ In spite of / Despite Although + S + V = In spite of / Despite + N/V-ing. Although he has money, he feels unhappy. → Despite his money, he feels unhappy. Although the weather was bad, we went out. → Despite the bad weather, we went out.
13. Unless ⇔ If not Unless = If + not (ngược nghĩa). If you don't study, you will fail. → Unless you study, you will fail.
14. Mệnh đề quan hệ (Relative Clauses) Dùng đại từ quan hệ (who, whom, which, that, whose) để nối hai câu. The man is my uncle. He is standing there. → The man who is standing there is my uncle. This is the book. I read it yesterday. → This is the book which I read yesterday. The girl is very kind. Her mother is a teacher. → The girl whose mother is a teacher is very kind. Lưu ý: Mệnh đề quan hệ không xác định dùng dấu phẩy, không dùng "that".

📌 Nhóm 5: Câu điều kiện và câu ước

15. Câu điều kiện loại 1, 2, 3 Đổi giữa các dạng câu điều kiện hoặc chuyển sang cấu trúc khác. If you don't keep silent, you will wake the baby. → Keep silent, or you'll wake the baby up. If it hadn't been so late, I would have called you. → It was late, so I didn't call you.
16. Wish / Regret Wish + quá khứ đơn (hiện tại), wish + had + V3 (quá khứ). I am sorry I don't know her. → I wish I knew her. I am sorry I didn't learn English when I was younger. → I wish I had learned English when I was younger.

📌 Nhóm 6: Cấu trúc đặc biệt

17. Câu chủ động bị động kép It + is/was + said/believed/reported... + that + S + V ⇔ S + is/was + said/believed/reported... + to V (hiện tại) / to have + V3 (quá khứ). People say that he is a genius. → It is said that he is a genius. / He is said to be a genius. They reported that the prisoners escaped. → It was reported that the prisoners escaped. / The prisoners were reported to have escaped.
18. Advice / Suggestion "If I were you, I would..." = lời khuyên → advise + O + to V. "If I were you, I would take the job," said my room-mate. → My room-mate advised me to take the job. "Would you like to come to my party?" → invited + O + to N.
19. Cấu trúc Remember / Forget / Regret Remember + V-ing = nhớ đã làm (quá khứ), Remember + to V = nhớ phải làm (tương lai). I don't remember that I met him. → I don't remember meeting him. Remember to lock the door. → Don't forget to lock the door.
Ví dụ tổng hợp:
He lost his job three months ago. → It is three months since he lost his job.
She took a taxi, but she arrived late. → Although she took a taxi, she arrived late.
I have never seen such a beautiful painting. → It is the most beautiful painting I have ever seen.
The girl is very kind. Her mother is a teacher. → The girl whose mother is a teacher is very kind.
📌 Lưu ý: Khi viết lại câu, cần đảm bảo giữ nguyên ý nghĩa, thay đổi cấu trúc ngữ pháp nhưng không thay đổi thì và thông tin chính. Đặc biệt chú ý đến các cặp cấu trúc tương đương như: because/because of, although/despite, so...that/such...that, too...to/enough...to.

🎯 Trắc nghiệm

30 câu · Chọn đáp án đúng nhất

✅ Chọn đáp án có nghĩa gần nhất hoặc viết lại đúng câu gốc. Sau khi nộp bài, hệ thống sẽ chấm điểm và hiển thị giải thích.

Câu 1
No other metal is as expensive as gold.
📘 Giải thích: So sánh nhất: No other + N + as + adj + as N = N + is the most + adj.
Câu 2
People use computers for various purposes.
📘 Giải thích: Bị động hiện tại đơn: S + is/are + V3.
Câu 3
Sally decided not to do her homework and went to a night club.
📘 Giải thích: Instead of + V-ing = thay vì làm gì.
Câu 4
He lost his job three months ago.
📘 Giải thích: It is + khoảng thời gian + since + S + V(quá khứ).
Câu 5
You must never take your helmet off while you are riding a motorcycle.
📘 Giải thích: must never take off = must be worn at all times.
Câu 6
They wanted to apologize for their behavior; that's why they paid for dinner.
📘 Giải thích: in order to + V = để làm gì (mục đích).
Câu 7
Jerry said, "I'm studying English a lot at the moment."
📘 Giải thích: Tường thuật: lùi thì (am → was), đổi I → he.
Câu 8
When I was a child, we would go to the local park every Saturday afternoon.
📘 Giải thích: would (thói quen quá khứ) = used to + V.
Câu 9
He found it extremely difficult to learn this subject.
📘 Giải thích: It + be + adj + for + O + to V = S + find it + adj + to V.
Câu 10
Although she took a taxi, Susan arrived late for the party.
📘 Giải thích: Although + S + V = In spite of + V-ing / N.
Câu 11
Well, this tea is too hot for me to drink now.
📘 Giải thích: Wish + quá khứ đơn (mong ước trái với hiện tại).
Câu 12
The new hospital is bigger than the old one.
📘 Giải thích: A is bigger than B = B is smaller than A.
Câu 13
My French friend finds driving on the left difficult.
📘 Giải thích: Finds + V-ing + difficult = is not used to + V-ing.
Câu 14
"Have you seen my gloves anywhere, Eric?" asked Mrs. Noble.
📘 Giải thích: Tường thuật câu hỏi Yes/No: asked if + S + had + V3 (lùi thì).
Câu 15
I don't remember that I met him in London.
📘 Giải thích: remember + V-ing = nhớ đã làm gì (đã xảy ra).
Câu 16
Mt. Everest is the highest mountain in the world. It's in the Himalayas.
📘 Giải thích: Dùng mệnh đề quan hệ không xác định: N, ... , ...
Câu 17
"If I were you, I would take the job," said my room-mate.
📘 Giải thích: "If I were you..." = lời khuyên → advised + O + to V.
Câu 18
"I will pay back the money, Gloria," said Ivan.
📘 Giải thích: "will pay back" = lời hứa → promised to + V.
Câu 19
The children couldn't go swimming because the sea was too rough.
📘 Giải thích: too + adj + for + O + to V = quá...đến nỗi không thể.
Câu 20
"Would you like to come to my birthday party, Sarah?" asked Frederic.
📘 Giải thích: "Would you like..." = lời mời → invited + O + to N.
Câu 21
The sooner we solve this problem, the better it will be for all concerned.
📘 Giải thích: So sánh kép = câu điều kiện loại 1.
Câu 22
"We'll go camping as long as the weather is good."
📘 Giải thích: as long as = if (với điều kiện).
Câu 23
It is reported that the prisoners escaped by means of a helicopter.
📘 Giải thích: Bị động kép: S + is/are reported to have + V3.
Câu 24
All of the students but Peter went to the meeting.
📘 Giải thích: All of the students but Peter = Except for Peter.
Câu 25
Keep silent or you'll wake the baby up.
📘 Giải thích: Keep silent or... = If you don't keep silent, you will...
Câu 26
Nam won the scholarship; he studied well.
📘 Giải thích: Dùng mệnh đề quan hệ không xác định với who.
Câu 27
The day was so cold that we stayed indoors.
📘 Giải thích: such + a/an + adj + N + that + clause.
Câu 28
They usually walk in the park after dinner.
📘 Giải thích: Usually (hiện tại) = are used to + V-ing.
Câu 29
Although he has a lot of money, he feels unhappy.
📘 Giải thích: Although + S + V = Despite + N.
Câu 30
I am sorry that I didn't learn English when I was younger.
📘 Giải thích: Wish + had + V3 (ước về quá khứ).
