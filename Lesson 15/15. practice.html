✍️ Bài 15: Luyện tập Câu tường thuật

Reported Speech Practice
📖 Xem lý thuyết

⏪ Luyện tập: Lùi thì & Không lùi thì

✅ Chọn đáp án đúng để hoàn thành câu tường thuật.

1. "I am tired," she said. → She said ______ tired.

⏪ Hiện tại đơn → quá khứ đơn: am/is → was.

2. "I will call you," he told me. → He told me he ______ call me.

⏪ will → would.

3. "I have finished," she said. → She said she ______ finished.

⏪ Hiện tại hoàn thành → quá khứ hoàn thành (had + V3).

4. "I don't know," he said. → He said he ______ know.

⏪ Hiện tại đơn phủ định → quá khứ đơn phủ định.

5. "We are going to the cinema," they said. → They said they ______ to the cinema.

⏪ Hiện tại tiếp diễn → quá khứ tiếp diễn.

6. "I can swim," she said. → She said she ______ swim.

⏪ can → could.

7. "I must go now," he said. → He said he ______ go then.

⏪ must → had to.

8. "I am a doctor," he said. → He said that he ______ a doctor.

⏪ Hiện tại đơn → quá khứ đơn.

9. "I will study harder," he said. → He said he ______ study harder.

⏪ will → would.

10. "I have been waiting," she said. → She said she ______ been waiting.

⏪ Hiện tại hoàn thành tiếp diễn → quá khứ hoàn thành tiếp diễn.

11. "I arrived early," he said. → He said he ______ early.

⏪ Quá khứ đơn có thể giữ nguyên hoặc lùi thành quá khứ hoàn thành.

12. "The Earth goes around the Sun," the teacher said. → The teacher said the Earth ______ around the Sun.

🌍 Sự thật hiển nhiên → không lùi thì.

13. "I am 18 years old," she said. (vẫn 18 tuổi) → She said she ______ 18 years old.

📌 Tình huống vẫn còn đúng → không lùi thì.

14. "I am busy," he says. → He ______ he is busy.

⏳ Động từ tường thuật ở hiện tại → không lùi thì.

15. "I'll call you," he said (vừa nói xong). → He said he ______ call me.

📢 Tường thuật ngay sau khi nói → có thể không lùi thì.

16. "I always drink coffee in the morning," he said. → He said he always ______ coffee in the morning.

🔄 Thói quen → không lùi thì.

17. "I live in London," she said (đã chuyển đi). → She said she ______ in London.

✘ Thông tin đã thay đổi → phải lùi thì.

18. "To be or not to be," the actor said. → The actor said that "to be or not to be" ______ a famous line.

📖 Trích dẫn văn học → không lùi thì.

19. "I will help you," he told me. → He told me he ______ help me.

⏪ will → would.

20. "I don't have any money," she said. → She said she ______ any money.

⏪ Hiện tại đơn phủ định → quá khứ đơn phủ định.

21. "I can speak French," he said. → He said he ______ speak French.

⏪ can → could.

22. "I must finish this," she said. → She said she ______ finish that.

⏪ must → had to.

23. "I am learning English," he said. → He said he ______ English.

⏪ Hiện tại tiếp diễn → quá khứ tiếp diễn.

24. "I have lived here for 5 years," she said. → She said she ______ there for 5 years.

⏪ Hiện tại hoàn thành → quá khứ hoàn thành.

25. "I will be there," he promised. → He promised he ______ be there.

⏪ will → would.

26. "The sun rises in the east," the teacher said. → The teacher said the sun ______ in the east.

🌅 Sự thật hiển nhiên → không lùi thì.

27. "I usually walk to school," he said. → He said he usually ______ to school.

🔄 Thói quen → không lùi thì.

28. "I am happy," she said (nhưng bây giờ không còn vui). → She said she ______ happy.

✘ Tình huống đã thay đổi → phải lùi thì.

29. "I can help you," she said. → She said she ______ help me.

⏪ can → could.

30. "I must be careful," he said. → He said he ______ be careful.

⏪ must → had to.

📢 Luyện tập: Mệnh lệnh & Câu hỏi tường thuật

✅ Chọn đáp án đúng để hoàn thành câu.

1. "Please close the door," she said. → She ______ me to close the door.

📢 Yêu cầu lịch sự → ask + O + to-V.

2. "Don't be late," he said. → He told me ______ be late.

📢 Mệnh lệnh phủ định: tell + O + not to-V.

3. "You should see a doctor," she said. → She advised me ______ a doctor.

📢 Lời khuyên: advise + O + to-V.

4. "Do you like coffee?" he asked. → He asked me ______ coffee.

❓ Yes/No question → ask + if/whether + S + V (lùi thì).

5. "Where do you live?" she asked. → She asked ______.

❓ Wh-question: ask + wh-word + S + V (lùi thì).

6. "Can you help me?" he asked. → He asked if I ______ help him.

❓ can → could (lùi thì).

7. "Please help me," she said. → She ______ me to help her.

📢 Yêu cầu: ask + O + to-V.

8. "Don't touch the painting," she said. → She told me ______ the painting.

📢 Mệnh lệnh phủ định: tell + O + not to-V.

9. "Did you see the film?" he asked. → He asked if I ______ the film.

❓ Quá khứ đơn → quá khứ hoàn thành (had + V3).

10. "What time is it?" he asked. → He asked what time ______.

❓ Wh-question: S + V (lùi thì), không đảo ngữ.

11. "Don't worry," he said. → He told me ______ worry.

📢 Mệnh lệnh phủ định: tell + O + not to-V.

12. "Are you coming?" she asked. → She asked if I ______ coming.

❓ Hiện tại tiếp diễn → quá khứ tiếp diễn.

13. "Please sit down," he said. → He asked us ______ down.

📢 Yêu cầu: ask + O + to-V.

14. "Why are you crying?" he asked. → He asked why I ______ crying.

❓ Wh-question: S + V (lùi thì).

15. "Don't forget to call," she said. → She reminded me ______ forget to call.

📢 Nhắc nhở: remind + O + not to-V.

16. "Can you open the window?" she asked. → She asked if I ______ open the window.

❓ can → could (lùi thì).

17. "Stop talking," the teacher said. → The teacher told us ______ talking.

📢 Mệnh lệnh: tell + O + to-V.

18. "What are you doing?" he asked. → He asked what I ______ doing.

❓ Wh-question: S + V (lùi thì).

19. "Please call me later," she said. → She asked me ______ her later.

📢 Yêu cầu: ask + O + to-V.

20. "Have you finished?" she asked. → She asked if I ______ finished.

❓ Hiện tại hoàn thành → quá khứ hoàn thành.

21. "Don't open the door," he said. → He told me ______ open the door.

📢 Mệnh lệnh phủ định: tell + O + not to-V.

22. "Where are you going?" she asked. → She asked where I ______ going.

❓ Wh-question: S + V (lùi thì).

23. "Please be quiet," she said. → She asked us ______ quiet.

📢 Yêu cầu: ask + O + to-V.

24. "Did you eat dinner?" he asked. → He asked if I ______ dinner.

❓ Quá khứ đơn → quá khứ hoàn thành.

25. "Don't smoke," the doctor said. → The doctor told me ______ smoke.

📢 Mệnh lệnh phủ định: tell + O + not to-V.

26. "How are you?" she asked. → She asked how I ______.

❓ Wh-question: S + V (lùi thì).

27. "Will you come?" he asked. → He asked if I ______ come.

❓ will → would (lùi thì).

28. "Please turn on the lights," she said. → She asked me ______ on the lights.

📢 Yêu cầu: ask + O + to-V.

29. "Don't eat too much," the doctor said. → The doctor advised me ______ eat too much.

📢 Lời khuyên phủ định: advise + O + not to-V.

30. "Who is that?" she asked. → She asked who ______.

❓ Wh-question: S + V (lùi thì), không đảo ngữ.
